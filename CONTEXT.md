# Ranger Outpost Hub

This context describes Ranger Outpost Hub, an independent public information service for Royal Rangers boys, leaders, churches, and outposts. It is not an official Royal Rangers platform and does not speak on behalf of Royal Rangers, the Assemblies of God, or their leaders.

## Community and organization

**Independent Service**:
Ranger Outpost Hub, operated separately from the official Royal Rangers organizations and their governing bodies.
_Avoid_: Official Royal Rangers platform, official directory

**Service Operator**:
The named person or legal entity accountable for Ranger Outpost Hub's privacy, security, disputes, expenses, and legal obligations even when routine operation is automated.
_Avoid_: Royal Rangers USA, autonomous software

**Operator Account**:
The single active Account entrusted initially to the project founder and subsequently to one named adult at a time to perform Ranger Outpost Hub's highest-privilege operational duties. Its credentials are never shared, ordinary accounts cannot grant themselves this role, it cannot disappear before a verified successor accepts a transfer, and its privileges renew every four years after two months' advance notice. Its authority is independent of its Current Outpost.
_Avoid_: Shared administrator, default login

**Operator Transfer**:
The current operator's deliberate handoff of the sole Operator Account responsibility to a verified adult successor by replacing that Account's name, verified email, and Current Outpost only after the successor accepts. The transfer preserves continuous control without creating a second Operator Account and immediately ends the former holder's application authority.
_Avoid_: Shared operator access, role deletion

**Operator Tenure**:
One person's period of control over the Operator Account. Immutable audit history attributes privileged actions to a numbered tenure so a successor is not credited with a predecessor's actions, while the predecessor's name and email are not disclosed to the successor.
_Avoid_: Shared operator history

**Account**:
An authenticated adult identity that may be granted permission to propose or publish changes in a future account-enabled release. The initial public release has no ordinary Accounts; only the sole Operator Account is securely provisioned to the project founder, and an Account never by itself establishes an official Royal Rangers role.
_Avoid_: Ranger record, membership record

**Initial Public Release**:
The public, read-only Ranger Outpost Hub containing only verified original information, directory and event facts entered by the Operator, and clearly identified external links. It excludes ordinary Accounts, member-only features, external editing, donations, uploads, and permission-gated reproduced material.
_Avoid_: Account-enabled release, licensed curriculum platform

**Permission-Gated Material**:
Content or data that requires written authorization for Ranger Outpost Hub to reproduce, translate, import, or distribute. It is linked at its authorized source or omitted unless permission already exists.
_Avoid_: Publicly reachable means reusable

**Birth Year**:
The private year supplied during an adult eligibility check. It is visible to no person, is not a full birth date, and is destroyed as soon as that request is evaluated.
_Avoid_: Public age, date of birth

**Adult Attestation**:
The registration confirmation that an Account applicant is at least 18 and understands that false information may cause rejection or deletion. It is a clear warning rather than a manipulative prompt and does not replace required legal review of the age-screening process.
_Avoid_: Guilt trip, verified birth date

**Adult Eligibility Record**:
The confirmation result, confirmation date, and accepted attestation version retained after the Birth Year is destroyed following the first successful sign-in.
_Avoid_: Retained birth year, birth date

**Privileged Access Event**:
An immutable, tenure-attributed audit entry recording an Operator Account action, including any exceptional access to private outpost material and the stated operational reason. It contains no Operator name or email.
_Avoid_: Unlogged administrator access

**Display Name**:
The first name or chosen name shown for an Account. Adult identity details collected for private verification are not part of the Display Name.
_Avoid_: Full legal name, public identity

**Website Credential**:
A secret or sign-in mechanism used only to access the Independent Service. It is never the password for the Account holder's email provider.
_Avoid_: Email password, mailbox password

**Claimed Position**:
The Royal Rangers relationship an Account holder selects: Parent/Guardian, Adult Leader, Outpost Coordinator, Pastor, Section/Division/Area Leader, District Leader, Regional Leader, National Leader, or FCF Leader. A future legally reviewed youth-account system may add Ranger/Boy; every selection personalizes the experience but grants no editing authority by itself.
_Avoid_: Permission, verified office

**Permission Grant**:
Verified authority for an Account to perform specified actions within specified Organizational Scopes. A Permission Grant is separate from a Claimed Position and never implies authority above or below its stated scope.
_Avoid_: Selected position, account type

**Membership Verification**:
Private confirmation that an Account holder belongs to a specified Outpost. Knowing an outpost number or selecting a position is not verification.
_Avoid_: Public roster, self-attestation

**Position Verification**:
Evidence supporting a Claimed Position before any related Permission Grant is issued. Verification follows the position's applicable Organizational Scope rather than granting authority throughout the service.
_Avoid_: Position selection

**Outpost Member**:
An Account holder whose relationship to a particular Outpost has been privately verified. An Outpost Member may view that Outpost's member-only information; membership is never exposed as a public roster.
_Avoid_: Public member, directory member

**Current Outpost**:
The single Outpost an Account holder presently identifies with, or No Current Outpost. For the Operator Account it is optional context that establishes neither membership nor authority; future ordinary-account membership is verified separately.
_Avoid_: Permanent outpost, multiple home outposts

**Onboarding Path**:
The Account-registration route chosen at the start: USA or International. Each path requests geography and organizational information appropriate to that context rather than forcing one location model on every Account.
_Avoid_: Universal registration form

**Outpost**:
A Royal Rangers group operated by a local church. An outpost may include one or more Program Groups and may have an official charter number.
_Avoid_: Troop, chapter

**Outpost Listing**:
A public description of an Outpost and its church, location, program groups, and public activities. It never means a roster or public record of individual Rangers.
_Avoid_: Outpost record, membership record

**Directory Submission**:
A private proposal to add or correct an Outpost Listing, received through safely configured intake or prepared for email fallback. The Operator verifies its proposed public facts against Source Documents; it is never an official charter, automatic publication, or canonical source.
_Avoid_: Outpost registration, charter application

**Duplicate Candidate**:
Evidence that a Directory Submission or staged candidate may refer to an existing Outpost Listing based on scoped identifiers, church/location facts, or sources. It requires an Operator decision and never causes an automatic merge.
_Avoid_: Confirmed duplicate, bare-number match

**Listing Verification**:
Evidence that an Outpost Listing corresponds to a currently operating church-based Royal Rangers program. Verification belongs to the Independent Service and must not be presented as official Royal Rangers approval.
_Avoid_: Official verification, charter approval

**Verification Cycle**:
One annual period of Listing Verification, tied to the exact Source Documents, Field Provenance, and accountable Operator Tenure that established it.
_Avoid_: Permanent verification, source freshness alone

**Verification Expired**:
The private state of a previously published Outpost Listing that was not reverified after its annual due date and 30-day grace period. It is removed from public display until verified again.
_Avoid_: Verified listing, closed outpost

**Archived Outpost**:
An Outpost Listing with a verified inactive or closed status and effective date. It is excluded from normal search but retained as a non-prominent historical record.
_Avoid_: Verification Expired, deleted outpost

**Coverage Review**:
A dated review of verified directory evidence for one civil jurisdiction or organizational district, recording current verified listing counts and explicit evidence gaps without asserting how many Outposts exist.
_Avoid_: Complete roster, expected total

**Published Fact**:
A statement displayed by Ranger Outpost Hub only after its supporting source and freshness have been verified. Missing or uncertain information is labeled as unverified or omitted rather than inferred as fact.
_Avoid_: Assumption, scraped claim

**Field Provenance**:
The source, verification date, and responsible change history attached to one published field rather than to an entire Outpost Listing or Event.
_Avoid_: Listing-wide source

**Source Document**:
One organizer-, church-, or publisher-controlled HTTPS page or document that may support several Published Facts across one or more records. Field Provenance identifies each exact fact the document supports.
_Avoid_: Repeated source copy, listing-wide source

**Ranger**:
A boy or young man participating in Royal Rangers. The Independent Service does not keep public records of individual Rangers.
_Avoid_: User, student, member record

**Leader**:
An adult who serves a Royal Rangers program or organizational scope. The term alone does not imply authority outside that person's assigned scope.
_Avoid_: Site administrator

**Organizational Scope**:
A distinct Royal Rangers area of responsibility, such as national, regional, district, or outpost. Responsibility within one scope does not grant general control over business belonging to another scope.
_Avoid_: Chain of command, management hierarchy

**Organizational Dispute**:
A disagreement about Royal Rangers ministry business within an Organizational Scope. The applicable Royal Rangers leaders handle it outside the Independent Service.
_Avoid_: Listing dispute

**Listing Dispute**:
A disagreement about information published by the Independent Service. The site's responsible editor resolves it from documented sources without claiming to exercise Royal Rangers organizational authority.
_Avoid_: Organizational dispute

**Outpost Coordinator**:
The leader responsible for the Royal Rangers program within an Outpost, subject to the authority of the local church's Pastor.
_Avoid_: Outpost owner

**Designated Pastor**:
The single verified Account assigned pastoral approval authority for an Outpost within Ranger Outpost Hub. The term describes a system responsibility and does not assert that the church has only one pastor.
_Avoid_: Any self-claimed pastor, all church pastors

## Programs and resources

**Program Group**:
One of the four USA grade-based Royal Rangers groups: Ranger Kids (K–2), Discovery Rangers (3–5), Adventure Rangers (6–8), or Expedition Rangers (9–12).
_Avoid_: Age group

**Program Profile**:
A country-specific description of locally verified Royal Rangers groups, ages or grades, advancement, awards, uniforms, terminology, languages, and official resource links.
_Avoid_: Universal USA curriculum

**Merit**:
A Royal Rangers achievement unit associated with one or more Program Groups and governed by official advancement rules.
_Avoid_: Badge, lesson

**Advancement Library**:
The public, searchable collection of verified USA and country-specific advancement metadata, original summaries, and clearly identified official resource links. It does not contain copied paid curriculum or saved personal progress.
_Avoid_: Curriculum mirror, progress tracker

**Handbook Listing**:
A verified publication record containing bibliographic details, an original summary, and authorized print or e-book purchase links. It is not a hosted, embedded, unlocked, or user-uploaded copy of the publication.
_Avoid_: Digital handbook copy

**Content Version**:
A dated, sourced state of an advancement, award, uniform, FCF, or Program Profile fact set. One version is identified as current while replaced versions remain clearly marked historical.
_Avoid_: Undated current content

**Event Scope**:
The Royal Rangers organizational area for which an Event is intended, such as national, regional, district, or outpost.
_Avoid_: Event level

**Event**:
A sourced occurrence with a host, scope, category, start and end in the organizer's time zone, public location status, intended audience, registration facts, lifecycle status, and Field Provenance.
_Avoid_: Unsourced announcement

**Event Series**:
A recurring concept whose dated Event occurrences remain separate historical records rather than being overwritten for each new occurrence.
_Avoid_: Reused event record

**Event Occurrence**:
One dated Event within an Event Series, with its own stable identity, sourced facts, lifecycle, and retained history.
_Avoid_: Current series date, overwritten event

**Outpost Calendar**:
An Outpost's member-only shared schedule of its own activities and the district, regional, or national Events it plans to attend. Only verified Outpost Members may view it, and only authorized editors may change it.
_Avoid_: Personal calendar

**Reference Calendar**:
A cross-outpost collection of documented national, regional, district, and FCF Events. It is distinct from an Outpost Calendar and does not indicate that any particular Outpost plans to attend.
_Avoid_: Outpost Calendar

**Meeting Plan**:
An Outpost leader's private agenda containing a date, title, theme, timed blocks, original notes, authorized resource links, related Merits, supplies, and an optional linked Event. It may be duplicated or exported but contains no attendance, Ranger identities, or copied curriculum.
_Avoid_: Republished curriculum, attendance record

**Saved Resource**:
An Account holder's private bookmark to an authorized advancement page, Event, Handbook Listing, or leader resource.
_Avoid_: Copied resource

**Deleted Account**:
An Account whose personal data, credentials, memberships, permissions, preferences, private calendars, bookmarks, and Meeting Plans have been destroyed after the retention period. It cannot be restored; the person must create and verify a new Account, while required editorial history identifies the former actor only as Deleted Account.
_Avoid_: Suspended account, restorable account

## Geography and affiliations

**RRI World Region**:
A Royal Rangers International organizational grouping that may differ from ordinary continental geography.
_Avoid_: Continent

**National Program**:
A country's self-governing Royal Rangers ministry and its locally adapted organization and program.
_Avoid_: International district, USA program copy

**Country-Defined Organizational Unit**:
A verified unit within a National Program, using that program's own name and depth, such as region, district, network, province, or area.
_Avoid_: Universal district

**Civil Location**:
The Outpost's physical place, expressed using the locally appropriate civil units such as country, territory, state, province, or municipality.
_Avoid_: Royal Rangers region

**Organizational Affiliation**:
A typed association between an Outpost and a Royal Rangers organizational unit, including language-specific districts or regions when applicable. Multiple affiliations may coexist and do not form a single management hierarchy.
_Avoid_: Parent organization

**FCF Affiliation**:
An Outpost's verified participation in the Frontiersmen Camping Fellowship, optionally including its FCF chapter and territory.
_Avoid_: Geographically inferred FCF participation

**FCF Territory**:
One of the eight USA FCF geographic units corresponding to a USA English-language Royal Rangers region.
_Avoid_: FCF region

**FCF Activity Status**:
An Outpost's reported FCF activity expressed as Yes, No, or Not Verified. No means inactivity was affirmatively established; missing evidence is always Not Verified.
_Avoid_: Boolean FCF flag, assumed inactivity

**FCF Availability**:
Whether a National Program has verified FCF participation: Available, Not Offered, or Not Verified. Outpost-level FCF activity is shown only where FCF is Available.
_Avoid_: Assumed global FCF

**Hub Outpost ID**:
Ranger Outpost Hub's stable internal identity for one Outpost Listing. Published outpost numbers remain optional, externally assigned identifiers scoped to their National Program and organizational unit.
_Avoid_: Bare outpost number

**Country Coverage Status**:
The verified state of international data: program not verified, country information available but directory incomplete, accepting verified submissions, or verified directory maintained by named local editors.
_Avoid_: Empty country, presumed absence

## Event stewardship

**Event Source**:
The organizer-controlled page or document supporting an Event's published facts, accompanied by the date those facts were last checked.
_Avoid_: Unsourced event listing

**Event Conflict**:
A documented disagreement between sources about an Event. An authorized editor for the Event's scope may correct the published facts while preserving the conflicting evidence and resolution history.
_Avoid_: Organizational Dispute

**Event Editor**:
A verified Leader with a Permission Grant to publish or correct Reference Calendar Events only within specified Event Scopes. Rangers and parents cannot edit Events.
_Avoid_: Any Account holder, global calendar editor

**Local Event Override**:
An Outpost-specific addition or correction that affects only its private Outpost Calendar and never changes the underlying Reference Calendar Event.
_Avoid_: Public event correction

**Automated Update Draft**:
A proposed data change created by monitoring an approved source. It preserves provenance and may publish automatically only when both the source and transformation have been approved; ambiguous or disputed changes require an authorized editor.
_Avoid_: Unsourced scrape, autonomous editorial decision

**Freshness Queue**:
The Operator's private worklist of facts approaching verification expiry, broken source links, conflicting Event facts, completed Events, and geographic coverage gaps. Automation adds drafts and reminders, while the Operator controls publication in the Initial Public Release.
_Avoid_: Automatic publication queue

## Data-use boundaries

**Operational Analytics**:
Minimal measurements needed to keep Ranger Outpost Hub reliable and secure. They exclude advertising, cross-site behavioral tracking, public profiles, sale of data, and monetization based on Account activity.
_Avoid_: Behavioral analytics, advertising profile
