# International directory initial cohort

Research date: **2026-08-13**

## Scope and evidence boundary

This note proposes a bounded Slice 15 cohort from current public pages controlled by Royal Rangers International (RRI), a national Royal Rangers program, or the named local church. It is not a global registry or a claim that any reviewed directory is complete. Every candidate must still enter private staging and receive human source review before draft conversion or publication.

The retained cohort has **9 local-unit candidates in 5 countries and 3 RRI displayed groupings**: Malaysia (2), Germany (1), United Kingdom (1), Finland (2), and South Africa (3). The cohort deliberately exercises compound identifiers, a numbered `Stammposten`, an identifier-less church unit, Finnish `tukikohta` terminology and Unicode place names, and South African `WP` identifiers. Personal leader names, phone numbers, email addresses, youth data, map coordinates, and source-only contact details were not transcribed.

### Publication interpretation

- The two Malaysia records are the only Slice 14 local fixtures reverified to current-operation quality. The national page supplies the exact identifiers, while current church-controlled pages independently establish that Royal Rangers still operates at those churches. The national directory's own `Updated on March 2020` notice means it is not evidence of current completeness [MY2].
- `RR116` replaces `RR150` as the usable Germany candidate. `RR150` remains a historical schema example only. The national-office-hosted January 2025 vacancy PDF explicitly says `Royal Rangers Stammposten 116 Düsseldorf` meets every Friday and belongs to Jesus-Haus; its advertised role began 2025-08-01 [DE2].
- `Wales 01` remains historical and draft-only. No current first-party page found during this pass ties that identifier to an operating unit. Lakeside Church Southport is usable without an invented identifier [GB2, GB3].
- South Africa's locator exposes coordinates, but coordinates are intentionally excluded. Its page also exposes a Swaziland lead; that lead is not retained because it supplies no local church or usable public address [ZA2].

| Finland | country information available but directory incomplete; accepting verified submissions | The national directory names 12 `tukikohdat`, but this bounded cohort retains only two entries with explicit current schedules. The page does not claim completeness or publish identifiers for them. |
| South Africa | country information available but directory incomplete; accepting verified submissions | The national site says it currently has 9 active outposts, but the locator produces 9 map entries with `WP9` split into two age/venue entries. That ambiguity prevents a completeness claim and requires human review before treating entries as distinct outposts. |

No country qualifies as a `verified directory maintained by named local editors`: none of the reviewed sources publishes both an authoritative completeness claim and a named update process. FCF is `available` at Malaysia's National Program level only [MY6]. Germany, UK, Finland, and South Africa remain `not-verified`; absence of reviewed evidence is not evidence that FCF is unavailable. No local candidate has verified active FCF status.

## Reproducible review procedure

1. Check RRI membership/grouping at [R1], preserving its displayed country and grouping strings.
2. Open the national source and the cited local church source where two-source binding is required. Search snippets alone are not evidence.
3. Capture only fields directly present on the cited page, with check date `2026-08-13`. Do not copy personal contacts or coordinates.
4. If an identifier, church, subdivision, organization affiliation, or FCF state is absent, omit it or record Not Verified. Never derive a hierarchy from an identifier.
5. Stage a bounded private manifest. A human Operator must compare both pages, decide duplicates/corrections, convert to a canonical draft, preview, and separately publish.
