## Transcription rules

- A source ID in a fact cell resolves to the exact HTTPS URL, source label, and check date in the source register below.
- “Candidate” means that a current, ordinarily public, first-party church page presents Royal Rangers as that church's ministry. It does not mean that national charter status, meeting status, or every publishable field has been verified.
- District/region values are map classifications, not claims made by the local page. Virginia rows remain district-unspecified because the cited map places Virginia ministries in the Northeast region but does not let this pass establish the precise Potomac/Appalachian boundary for each church.
- Street address, meeting time, age/grade details, program status, contact details, and personal data are intentionally omitted. No social post, third-party directory/map/review, or search-result snippet is used as the evidence of record.
- FCF was not established by the reviewed sources, so every row defaults to **Not verified**.

Those pages established organizational coverage or led to searches, but were not treated as local outpost records unless a church-owned source independently supported the row.

The repeatable query pattern was `site:<district-or-church-domain> "Royal Rangers"`, followed by direct opening of the first-party page and a currentness check. Rejected leads included search-snippet-only results, pages that could not be opened directly, stale event-only references, pages that named Royal Rangers only historically, social-only references, and pages without a church/city binding. The national locator may still be used later for one-record-at-a-time human verification, but it was not a cohort discovery source here.
