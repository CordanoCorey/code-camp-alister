Checked: **2026-08-13**

Status: **candidate evidence, not publication-ready data**. This bounded cohort contains 52 currently verifiable Royal Rangers candidates in the Southwest, South Central, Gulf, and Southeast national regions. It covers 14 named geographic districts. The South Texas rows come from the district-owned page's current-outpost list [D1]; every other row is supported by an ordinarily public, church-owned page that was opened and read. District/region classification follows the official national region map [M1]. The national locator was not used for cohort discovery, queried in bulk, or accessed through its undocumented endpoint.

## Transcription rules

- A source ID in a fact cell resolves to the exact HTTPS URL, source label, and check date in the source register below.
- “Candidate” means that a current first-party district page lists the church as a current outpost, or a current first-party church page presents Royal Rangers as that church's ministry. It does not establish national charter status or independently verify every optional field.
- District/region values outside South Texas are map classifications [M1], not claims copied from the local page. No language district is inferred. Florida rows are split only where the city is plainly on the respective side of the official West Florida/Peninsular Florida boundary; no ambiguous Florida candidate is included.
- Street address, ZIP, meeting details, age/grade details, Program Groups, public contacts, and personal data are intentionally omitted. Scoped outpost numbers appear only where the supporting source states them.
- No social post, third-party directory/map/review, or search-result snippet is evidence of record. No source prose is copied into candidate facts.
- FCF activity was not established for any candidate, so every row defaults to **Not verified**. District/region geography is not evidence of local FCF activity.

### Searches and explicit coverage gaps

The repeatable discovery pattern was `"Royal Rangers" church <state> ministry`, followed by direct opening of the first-party page and a live/currentness check. District-list searches used `Royal Rangers district outpost locator <district-or-state>`. Exact bounded searches included Arizona, California, Hawaii, Colorado, New Mexico, Kansas, Oklahoma, Texas, southern Missouri, Arkansas, Tennessee, Mississippi, Louisiana, Alabama, Georgia, North Carolina, South Carolina, and Florida. Leads were rejected when only a snippet, third-party directory/map/review, social post, historical publication, inaccessible page, or current church page without Royal Rangers remained.

- **Southwest:** no retained candidate from Northern California/Nevada, Southern California, or Rocky Mountain. The Southern Pacific district outpost page exposed placeholder/template content rather than usable local records; the Southern California page linked an outpost search but did not itself supply candidate facts. This does not mean no outposts exist.
- **South Central:** no retained candidate from Kansas, North Texas, or West Texas. West Texas & Plains search results belonged to the separate Spanish-language overlay and were not relabeled as a geographic West Texas district. New Mexico and Oklahoma yielded only one retained church-owned page each in this bounded pass.
- **Gulf:** no retained candidate from Southern Missouri. Louisiana, Mississippi, and Tennessee district sites established active district ministries but did not expose a current reusable local roster, so only church-owned pages were retained.
- **Southeast:** no retained candidate from South Carolina or Puerto Rico. A South Carolina IPHC charter list named outposts but did not bind every record to a city/state without reproducing personal-contact pages, so it was not used to inflate the cohort. No usable current first-party Puerto Rico local listing was found during this pass.
- The South Texas district page still listed Glad Tidings Assembly of God in Houston as Outpost 41, while the current successor church identifies a 2022 replant/name change elsewhere. That conflicted row was excluded rather than resolved by convenience. Locator or roster absence was never treated as closure evidence.
