## Source interpretation notes

- The South Texas district page calls these entries “current outposts” by organizing them in its active section list, but it does not provide street addresses, meeting schedules, Program Groups, or local FCF status. Those fields require separate church-owned evidence. ([South Texas Royal Rangers outpost locator](https://www.stxroyalrangers.com/outpost-locator/))
- The district list is not sufficient evidence of a current host identity: it still associates Outpost 41 with Glad Tidings in Houston even though the congregation says it renamed and relocated to Cypress in 2022. ([South Texas Royal Rangers outpost locator](https://www.stxroyalrangers.com/outpost-locator/); [Discovery Church Cypress history](https://discoverychurchcypress.com/about/who-we-are))
- The national map supports district-to-region relationships, not local outpost activity. Likewise, the national territory table supports a territory-to-region relationship, not an inference that any listed outpost participates in FCF. ([Official Royal Rangers USA region/district map](https://royalrangers.com/~/-/media/F5F81CB2C829419A82139A817D8073A2.ashx); [Royal Rangers USA territory/region table](https://royalrangers.com/en/news/General-News/2023/2023-06-Territorial-Rendezvous?D=%7B39CB0294-58F1-4250-AB82-25E3BF8466D2%7D))
- For the public `activeFcf` field, all five records should remain **Not verified** (`null`), not **No**. None of the reviewed first-party outpost, church, or district pages makes an explicit local FCF-activity statement. ([LifeChange Outpost #1](https://www.royalrangersgreenville.org/aboutus); [Angleton First Royal Rangers](https://angletonfirst.org/ministries/royal-rangers/); [South Texas Royal Rangers outpost locator](https://www.stxroyalrangers.com/outpost-locator/))
- Targeted searches of the current national locator returned **Outpost #070** at First Assembly of God in Angleton. They did not return matching records for the other four candidates. The locator itself warns that parent-church affiliation or an expired charter can cause omissions, so these non-results are not proof that an outpost is inactive. ([Royal Rangers USA outpost locator](https://royalrangers.com/locator))
