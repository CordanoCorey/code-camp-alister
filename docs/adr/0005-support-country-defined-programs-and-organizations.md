# Support country-defined programs and organizational structures

The international system models an RRI world region, a self-governing National Program, variable-depth Country-Defined Organizational Units, and local Outposts while keeping civil geography separate. Each country also has its own Program Profile, because international ministries may not use USA districts, Program Groups, advancement, merits, or FCF; verified local facts must not be replaced by assumptions based on geography or the USA program.
