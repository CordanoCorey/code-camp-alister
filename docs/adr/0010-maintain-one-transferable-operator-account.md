# Maintain one transferable Operator Account

Ranger Outpost Hub securely provisions the sole Operator Account to the project founder and permits its current holder to transfer responsibility to one verified adult successor by replacing the account's name, verified email, and Current Outpost after acceptance. Its privileged session renews every four years after two months' notice, but the role is never automatically deleted or reassigned; transfer invalidates the predecessor's sessions and recovery methods, while numbered Operator Tenures preserve accurate immutable auditing without revealing the predecessor's identity to the successor. System-wide authority remains independent of Current Outpost, keeping single-person control continuous, recoverable, and accountable without becoming shared access.
