# Delete inactive accounts after six months

This decision's deletion-timing rule is superseded by ADR 0019. An ordinary Account receives a warning one calendar month before access is due; if that warning is accepted by the configured delivery provider and the Account is not renewed before expiry, Ranger Outpost Hub permanently destroys the Account six calendar months after confirmed provider acceptance, not six months after expiration. Returning later requires a new Account. Published facts remain independent, and only a real durable editorial reference may retain the literal anonymous attribution `Deleted Account` without the person's application identity.
