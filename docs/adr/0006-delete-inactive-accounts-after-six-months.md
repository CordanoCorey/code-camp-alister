# Delete inactive accounts after six months

An Account receives warning 30 days before its access expires; if it remains inactive, Ranger Outpost Hub permanently destroys its personal profile, credentials, memberships, permissions, preferences, and private content six months after expiration, so returning later requires a new Account. Published facts and necessary editorial timestamps remain, but the former actor is attributed only to `Deleted Account`, preserving source accountability without retaining the person's application identity.
