# Expire and delete ordinary Accounts after a delivery-based Renewal Notice

An ordinary Account receives a one-calendar-year Ordinary Access Term from activation. Its private Renewal Notice opens one calendar month before the exact due instant, and the holder may explicitly choose **Yes, renew for one year** only before expiry. Timely Renewal extends from the existing due instant, never from a page view or other passive activity, and changes no eligibility, membership, Claimed Position, Current Outpost, Permission Grant, email verification, or Operator state.

At the due instant, an unrenewed Account becomes an Expired Account: existing sessions are revoked, new ordinary sessions and private capabilities are blocked, and late renewal is unavailable. Public browsing remains account-free. Lifecycle maintenance acts under the system maintenance identity rather than an Operator Tenure, and the sole Operator Account remains governed by its separate four-year non-deleting lifecycle.

The Deletion Deadline is six calendar months after the configured email provider accepted the current term's Renewal Notice. A delivery attempt, rejection, timeout, or repeated failure does not start that clock. Repeated failure coalesces one private Automation Alert and fails closed. Successful delivery and each lifecycle transition use durable per-term idempotency, optimistic version checks, bounded leased maintenance, and sanitized non-PII outcomes.

At or after the deadline, one guarded transition rechecks the current term and version and permanently removes credentials, sessions, verification and recovery material, email previews, eligibility, profile, claims, lifecycle delivery detail, and private ordinary content. There is no soft-deleted user, recoverable suspension, or end-user restore. A returning person creates and verifies a wholly new Account. Public facts remain independent; only an existing durable editorial reference that truly requires actor continuity may be rewritten to the literal anonymous label `Deleted Account`, with no name, email, auth subject, or publicly correlatable former-user identity retained.
