# Grow the U.S. directory through verified proposals

Directory Submissions and source-controlled population manifests are private proposals, never Published Facts. Because no authorized complete national bulk source exists, the Operator verifies each proposed field against an ordinarily public Source Document, records exact Field Provenance under an active Operator Tenure, and grows coverage incrementally without claiming completeness; source manifests may stage idempotent drafts but cannot publish or bypass the canonical write and audit path.

Stable Hub Outpost IDs, source identifiers, scoped number/district/campus labels, church/location facts, and source URLs provide duplicate evidence; a bare outpost number never identifies or automatically merges a listing. Annual Listing Verification lasts one calendar year with a 30-day grace period, after which Verification Expired removes the listing from ordinary public results without asserting closure. Archival instead requires affirmative inactive or closed evidence and preserves historical identity. Private reply email and free-form notes are temporary and are scrubbed after disposition or retention review while non-personal disposition history remains.
