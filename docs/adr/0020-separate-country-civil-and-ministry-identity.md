# Separate country, civil geography, and ministry organization

International directory records use an ISO country identity, an optional country-labeled Civil Subdivision, and independently sourced Ministry Organization Units connected through overlapping Affiliation Scopes. Source-native local-unit labels and identifiers are preserved verbatim; the service does not translate them into a universal U.S. region/district/FCF hierarchy, because national programs demonstrably use different structures and missing evidence must remain Not Verified.

## Consequences

Bare local-unit numbers are unique only within a National Program and optional campus suffix. FCF is an optional affiliation whose availability and activity remain Not Verified unless a country source explicitly establishes them. Country fixtures may remain private drafts when a source proves the model shape but not current operation.
